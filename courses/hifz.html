What is Hifz?

Hifz is the memorization of the whole Quran, verse by verse, until it can be recited from memory. It is a long journey that takes patience and steady daily work — and a teacher who listens to every verse, corrects mistakes, and makes sure nothing memorised is forgotten.

Our Hifz course is taught live, one-to-one, by a certified Hafiz or Hafiza tutor. There are no group classes: the tutor's full attention is on one student for the whole lesson.

Who is this course for?

  • Kids and adults who already read the Quran fluently and want to memorise it.
  • Students who have memorised some parts before and want to continue, or to strengthen what they already know.
  • Families ready for a daily commitment — Hifz needs regular classes and daily revision at home.

You should be able to read the Quran fluently before starting Hifz. If you are not there yet, start with Nazra first — your tutor will check your level in the trial classes.

How Hifz works: Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil

Every Hifz class follows the traditional three-part revision system, so new memorization is always backed up by revision:

  • Sabaq — the new lesson. A new portion memorised each day and recited to the tutor, who corrects it before it is learned wrong.
  • Sabqi — recent revision. The lessons memorised over the last few days and weeks, recited again so they stay fresh.
  • Manzil — older revision. A portion of what was memorised long ago, cycled through regularly, so the whole memorised part stays strong and nothing is lost.

Without all three parts, memorization fades. With them, the student keeps moving forward while holding on to everything already learned.

Class format

Hifz classes are live and one-to-one, 45 minutes each, 5 days a week, on Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp — whichever you prefer — at a time that suits your timezone. Your tutor is a certified Hafiz or Hafiza. Female tutors are available for kids and sisters, at no extra cost. Parents are kept informed about what their child is learning.

How long does full Hifz take?

Full Hifz takes years for most students. How long it takes depends on the student's age, how much new lesson they memorise each day, and how faithfully they do their daily revision. Some students move faster than others, and that is normal. Your tutor will set a realistic pace after the trial classes — we make no exact promise about a finish date, because a promise like that would not be honest.

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need to read fluently before starting Hifz?

Yes. Hifz builds on fluent reading. If your child is still learning to read, they should finish Noorani Qaida and Nazra first. Your tutor will check their level in the trial classes.

How much revision is needed every day?

Daily revision is part of Hifz — the Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil system only works if the student revises between classes. Your tutor will set the daily revision for your child's level.

Can adults memorise the Quran too?

Yes. Many of our Hifz students are adults. The pace is set for the student — adults often memorise a smaller new lesson each day, with the same revision system.

What happens if we miss a class?

Life happens. Tell your tutor and the plan is adjusted — the missed lesson is picked up in the next class, and revision keeps going so nothing is lost.
