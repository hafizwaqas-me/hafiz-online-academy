Nazra Quran Reading Classes Online

Read the Quran fluently, page by page, with a live tutor listening and correcting you — the step after Noorani Qaida, on the way to Tajweed and Hifz.

Book 3 Free Classes

What is Nazra?

Nazra means reading the Quran by looking at the text — reading the whole Quran from the Mushaf, from the first page to the last. In this course you read aloud to your tutor in every lesson, so mistakes are heard and corrected straight away, instead of going unnoticed.

Nazra is the stage between Noorani Qaida and Tajweed: you already know the letters and joining rules, and now you build speed, flow and confidence until reading feels natural.

Who is this course for?

  • Students who have finished Noorani Qaida and are ready to read the Quran itself.
  • Students who can read, but slowly — sounding out words letter by letter — and want to read fluently.
  • Adults returning to the Quran after a long break who want to rebuild their reading with a tutor.

If you are not sure of your level, your tutor will check it in the free trial classes and start you at the right place. If you cannot read yet, start with Noorani Qaida first.

What you will learn

  • Fluent, confident reading of the Quran, page by page.
  • Correct pronunciation, with your tutor correcting you as you read.
  • A steady reading pace — no more stopping at every word.
  • Where to pause and where to continue while reading.
  • Working through the Quran in order, towards completing a full reading.

Class format

Classes are live and one-to-one, 30 minutes each, on Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp — whichever you prefer. You can choose 2, 3 or 5 days a week, at a time that suits your timezone. Female tutors are available for kids and sisters, at no extra cost. Parents are kept informed about what their child is learning.

How long does it take?

It depends on where you start and how often you attend. A student who reads slowly at first usually becomes fluent over a few months of regular classes, and completing a full reading of the Quran takes longer — your tutor will set a realistic plan for you in the trial classes and adjust it as you progress. We make no fixed promise about a finish date.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to finish Noorani Qaida before Nazra?

Yes, Nazra builds on Noorani Qaida. You should know the letters, Harakat and joining rules before you start reading the Quran itself. If you have not learned them yet, start with our Noorani Qaida course.

I can read, but very slowly. Is this course right for me?

Yes — that is exactly what Nazra is for. Reading aloud to a tutor every lesson is how slow, careful reading becomes fluent reading.

How long will it take to finish reading the whole Quran?

It depends on your starting level and how many days a week you study. Your tutor will set a plan for you after the trial classes. There is no fixed timetable — steady, regular reading is what matters.

Will I learn Tajweed in this course?

Your tutor will correct your pronunciation as you read. When you are reading fluently and ready to study the rules in depth, you can move on to our Tajweed course.
