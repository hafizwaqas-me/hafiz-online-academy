What is Tajweed?

Tajweed is the set of rules for reciting the Quran correctly — giving every letter its due, pronouncing each sound from its proper place, and observing the rules of joining, stretching and stopping. It is how the Quran was revealed and recited, and learning it turns plain reading into correct, beautiful recitation.

In this course you do not just memorise rules: you apply them while reading, with your tutor listening and correcting you in every lesson, until correct recitation becomes a habit.

Who is this course for?

  • Students who can already read the Quran and want to recite it correctly.
  • Students who read fluently but know their pronunciation needs correcting.
  • Adults and children who want to improve their recitation, whether for daily reading or for Hifz.

You should be able to read the Quran before starting Tajweed. If you are still learning to read, start with Noorani Qaida or Nazra first — your tutor will check your level in the trial classes.

What you will learn

  • Makharij — the articulation points: exactly where each letter sound comes from, so similar letters are no longer confused.
  • Noon Sakinah and Tanween — the rules of Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab and Ikhfa, and when each one applies.
  • Meem Sakinah — the rules of Ikhfa Shafawi, Idgham Shafawi and Izhar Shafawi.
  • Madd — lengthening: which sounds are stretched, and for how long.
  • Stopping rules (Waqf) — where to stop, where to continue, and how to restart correctly.
  • Applying the rules while reading — reciting passages with your tutor correcting you, until the rules feel natural.

Class format

Classes are live and one-to-one, 30 minutes each, on Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp — whichever you prefer. You can choose 2, 3 or 5 days a week, at a time that suits your timezone. Female tutors are available for kids and sisters, at no extra cost. Parents are kept informed about what their child is learning.

How long does it take?

It depends on your starting level and how often you attend. Students usually spend a few months learning the main rules and then keep applying them while reading — Tajweed improves steadily with regular practice and correction, and your tutor will set a plan for you after the trial classes. We make no fixed promise about a finish date.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know Tajweed before joining?

No. This course teaches Tajweed from the basics. You only need to be able to read the Quran — your tutor will check your level in the trial classes and start from there.

Is Tajweed only for advanced students?

No. Tajweed is for anyone who can read the Quran and wants to recite it correctly. The course starts with the basic rules and builds up step by step.

What is the difference between Nazra and Tajweed?

Nazra is about reading the Quran fluently. Tajweed is about reading it correctly — with every letter pronounced from its proper place and every rule observed. Most students do Nazra first, then Tajweed.

Will the tutor correct me while I read?

Yes. Every class is live and one-to-one, so your tutor hears every verse you read and corrects your pronunciation straight away. That is the whole point of learning Tajweed with a teacher.
