What is Noorani Qaida?

Noorani Qaida is the beginner's primer for reading the Quran. It teaches the Arabic alphabet step by step — how each letter looks, how it sounds, and how letters join together to form words. It is the foundation every Quran reader starts with, and it is taught the same way to children and to adults who are starting from the beginning.

Our Noorani Qaida course is taught live, one-to-one, by a certified male or female tutor. There are no group classes and no recorded videos: the tutor listens to the student in every lesson and corrects pronunciation straight away.

Who is this course for?

  • Kids aged 4 and over who are starting to learn the Quran for the first time.
  • Adult beginners who never learned to read Arabic, or who want to start again from the basics.
  • Students who can recognise some letters but cannot yet read words with confidence.

No previous knowledge of Arabic is needed. If you can already read a little, your tutor will check your level in the free trial classes and start you at the right place.

What you will learn

  • The Arabic alphabet — every letter, its name and its sound.
  • Harakat — Fatha, Damma and Kasra — and Tanween.
  • How letters join together to form words.
  • Sukoon, Shaddah and the basics of Madd (stretching a sound).
  • Makharij basics — where each letter sound comes from in the mouth and throat.
  • Reading short words and sentences with confidence, ready to move on to Nazra.

Class format

Classes are live and one-to-one, 30 minutes each, on Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp — whichever you prefer. You can choose 2, 3 or 5 days a week, at a time that suits your timezone. Female tutors are available for kids and sisters, at no extra cost. Parents are kept informed about what their child is learning.

How long does it take?

Most students finish Noorani Qaida in a few months with regular classes, but it depends on the student's age, how often they attend, and how much they practise between lessons. Your tutor will set the pace for the student and move on only when each step is secure — we make no fixed promise about a finish date.

Frequently asked questions

What age can my child start Noorani Qaida?

Most children can start from around 4 or 5 years old, once they can sit and focus for a short lesson. We start young learners with the letters and sounds, at a gentle pace.

Does my child need to know Arabic first?

No. Noorani Qaida is designed for complete beginners. No previous knowledge of Arabic is needed — the course starts with the alphabet.

Can adults take this course?

Yes. Many of our students are adults learning to read the Quran for the first time, or starting again after a long break. The lessons are one-to-one, so the pace is set for you.

What do we need for the first lesson?

Just a device with Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp and a quiet place to sit. Your tutor will guide you through everything else in the first lesson.
