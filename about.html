About Hafiz Online Academy

An online Quran academy for families who want their children — and themselves — to learn the Quran properly, with a real teacher, from home.

What the academy is

Hafiz Online Academy is an online Quran academy. All classes are taught live, one-to-one, by certified male and female tutors — never group classes, and never recorded videos passed off as lessons. A student sits with one tutor for the whole lesson, on video, and the tutor listens, corrects and guides them throughout.

The academy exists for families living anywhere in the world who may not have a qualified Quran teacher nearby — in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and beyond — and for sisters learning from home who prefer a female teacher.

Who it is for

Our students are mainly kids and sisters, from all countries. That includes:

  • Kids starting with Noorani Qaida, often from around 4 or 5 years old, once they can sit and focus for a short lesson.
  • Sisters learning from home, including those who prefer a certified female tutor.
  • Adults returning to the Quran, or starting for the first time.
  • Hifz students who want a structured memorisation plan with a certified Hafiz or Hafiza.

How classes work

Classes are live video lessons on Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp — whichever the student prefers. A standard class is 30 minutes; Hifz classes are 45 minutes. Families choose a monthly plan of 2, 3 or 5 days a week, at times that suit their timezone. Before paying anything, every new student takes 3 free classes with their tutor.

Our approach

  • One-to-one, always. The tutor's full attention is on one student for the whole lesson.
  • Patient teaching. Tutors teach at the student's pace, repeat as needed, and keep lessons encouraging — especially for young learners.
  • A plan for every student. The tutor checks the student's level in the first class and sets a learning plan made for them, not a fixed course for everyone.
  • Regular progress updates. Parents are kept informed about what their child is learning and how they are progressing.
  • Certified, background-checked tutors. Tutors are Hafiz/Hafiza and Ijazah-certified, and background-checked before they teach.
