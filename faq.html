Frequently Asked Questions

Everything families ask us most about learning the Quran online with Hafiza Online Academy.

What age can children start Quran classes?

Most children can start once they can sit and focus for a short lesson, often from around 4 or 5 years old. We start young learners with Noorani Qaida — letters, sounds and correct pronunciation — before moving to reading.

Are the classes really one-to-one?

Yes. Every class is one student with one tutor, live on video. There are no group classes, so the tutor's full attention stays on your child for the whole lesson.

Can I choose a female teacher?

Yes. We have certified female tutors for kids and sisters. Just ask for a female teacher when you book your free trial — there is no extra cost.

Which platforms do you teach on?

Classes are held on Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp — whichever the student prefers. You choose your platform when you book, and you can change it later if you prefer a different one.

How do the 3 free trial classes work?

Book through the trial form or email us. We arrange 3 real classes with your tutor at times that suit you. No card is needed and there is no obligation to continue.

How much are the fees, and how do I pay?

Fees are in USD per month: $25 for 2 days a week (8 classes), $35 for 3 days a week (12 classes, most popular), $55 for 5 days a week (20 classes), and $65 for the Hifz program (5 days a week, 45-minute classes). There is no registration fee. Payment details are arranged by email after you choose a plan — contact us at hafizaonline.it@gmail.com and we will share the payment options available for your country.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a different plan — for example from 2 days a week to 3 days a week — at the start of a new month. Just email us before your next month begins.

What times are classes available? Do you cover my timezone?

We teach students worldwide — including the USA, UK, Canada and Australia — and arrange class times to suit your timezone. Tell us your preferred times when you book and we will match a tutor who is available then.

What does a student need for classes?

Just a device with a camera and microphone — a phone, tablet, laptop or computer — plus an internet connection and the app for your chosen platform (Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp). No special equipment or books are needed to start; your tutor will guide you on anything else.

What do I need before starting Hifz?

Hifz students should be able to read the Quran fluently, usually after completing Nazra with good Tajweed. If you are not sure whether you are ready, the tutor will check your level in the free trial classes and advise whether to start Hifz or to strengthen reading first.

Do students get a certificate?

If you need a certificate of completion for a course, contact us by email at hafizaonline.it@gmail.com and we will discuss it with you.

Is there a discount for siblings or family members?

Yes, a family discount is available on request when more than one child or family member is learning with us. Contact us and we will work out the discount for your family.

How do I contact the academy?

By email at hafizaonline.it@gmail.com, or through the contact form. We reply by email.
