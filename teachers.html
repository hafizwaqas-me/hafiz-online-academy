Our Quran Tutors

Certified male and female tutors who teach live, one-to-one, with patience and care — for kids and sisters, worldwide.

What makes a Hafiza Online Academy tutor

Every tutor at the academy is chosen for their knowledge of the Quran, their teaching ability and their character. We only work with tutors who meet all of these standards:

  • Hafiz / Hafiza. Our tutors have memorised the Quran themselves, so they teach from real experience — including our Hifz program.
  • Ijazah-certified. Tutors hold an Ijazah, a certified chain of authorisation to teach the Quran with correct Tajweed.
  • Background-checked. Every tutor is background-checked before they teach a single class.
  • Experienced with kids. Tutors are chosen for patient, friendly teaching that keeps young learners motivated.

Tutor profiles coming soon

Individual tutor profiles with qualifications and teaching areas are coming soon. In the meantime, the best way to meet your tutor is through the free trial — you will take 3 real classes with them before deciding anything.

Female tutors for kids and sisters

We have certified female tutors available for kids and sisters. Many families prefer a female teacher for their daughters or for themselves, and we are glad to arrange it — at no extra cost.

Just ask for a female teacher when you book your free trial, and we will match you with a qualified female tutor.

Male tutors

Certified male tutors are available for boys, adult male students, and any family that prefers a male teacher. Tell us your preference when you book and we will match accordingly.

How matching works

How we match you with a tutor

1

You tell us what you need

When you book the free trial, tell us the student's age, course (Noorani Qaida, Nazra, Tajweed or Hifz), preferred platform and whether you would like a male or female tutor.

2

We check your level

In your first free class, the tutor checks the student's current level — from the first letter to fluent reading — and sets a learning plan together with you.

3

You learn with the same tutor

You continue with the same tutor at your chosen days and times. If the match does not feel right, let us know and we will arrange a different tutor.

Meet your tutor with 3 free classes

Real lessons with your tutor, at a time that suits you. No card required, no obligation to continue.
