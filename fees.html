What is included — and what is not charged

  • Cancel anytime. There is no long-term contract. If you need to stop, let us know and your plan ends at the end of the paid month.
  • Family discount on request. If more than one child or family member is learning with us, contact us about a family discount.

Compared with other academies

Online Quran academies commonly charge around $30–$85 a month for similar one-to-one plans, with some charging extra for female tutors or registration. Our aim is to keep fees at the lower end of that range — from $25 a month — while keeping every class live, one-to-one, and taught by a certified tutor, with no registration fee and no extra charge for a female teacher.

Fees — common questions

How do I pay?

Payment details are arranged by email after you book your free trial and choose a plan. Contact us at hafizaonline.it@gmail.com and we will share the payment options available for your country.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a different plan — for example from 2 days a week to 3 days a week — at the start of a new month. Just email us before your next month begins.

Is there a discount for siblings or family members?

Yes, a family discount is available on request when more than one child or family member is learning with us. Contact us and we will work out the discount for your family.

What is the refund and cancellation policy?

There is no long-term contract and you can cancel anytime — your plan simply ends at the end of the month you have paid for. If you have a question about a payment or a refund, contact us by email and we will help you directly.

Are there any other charges?

No. There is no registration fee and no extra charge for a female tutor, for changing your class time, or for your chosen platform (Zoom, Skype or WhatsApp). You only pay your monthly plan fee.
